# Chapter 1 Lab: Cloud Identity, SSO, and Workforce Identity Federation

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Configure a Workforce Identity Federation pool and provider in the Google Cloud console mapping an external SAML IdP to IAM roles, then trace the sign-in flow end to end

## Key takeaway

Workforce Identity Federation handles external human identity access; Cloud Identity SSO and Directory Sync govern org-wide identity provisioning and authentication policy.
