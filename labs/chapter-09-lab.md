# Chapter 9 Lab: Binary Authorization, CVE Scanning, and Security Posture Management

**Estimated time:** 22 minutes

## Exercise

Configure a Binary Authorization policy requiring a build attestation before deployment to GKE, attempt to deploy an unattested image and observe the block, then create the attestation and redeploy successfully

## Key takeaway

Binary Authorization is the preventive deploy-time gate, while Security Health Analytics and posture management provide ongoing detective visibility into misconfigurations.
